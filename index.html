        How it works

        1. Choose a file

          Drop a video or an image. Zafronix reads its real size and checks that your browser can process it.

        2. Pick a model

          Choose the footage type and model size. The exact output resolution is shown before you start.

        3. Enhance and download

          Frames are decoded, enhanced on your GPU and encoded into a new file. Compare it with the original, then download.

        What it does

        Video to 1080p
        Videos smaller than 1080p are enhanced 2x. Sources between 540p and 1080p are enhanced 2x and then resized to fit 1080p.
        Images up to 8K
        2x in one pass, 4x in two passes, capped at 7680 by 4320 pixels. The output size is shown before you start.
        Private by design
        Decoding, enhancement and encoding all happen in this tab. The only downloads are this page and a small model file.
        Three model sizes
        Small, Medium and Large trade speed for sharpness. Weights for real-life footage, animation and 3D renders are included.
        Original audio kept
        Audio is copied into the new MP4 without re-encoding, when it is AAC, MP3 or Opus.
        Plain limits
        Files that are already 1080p or larger, rotated phone clips and unsupported codecs get a clear explanation instead of a silent failure.

        Questions

        Are my files uploaded anywhere?

        No. Your video or image is read, enhanced and encoded inside your browser, on your own device. This site only serves static files and includes no analytics or tracking scripts.

        Which browsers work?

        The tool needs WebGPU, and video also needs WebCodecs with H.264 encoding. The latest desktop Chrome or Edge is the safest choice. The status line above the upscaler shows exactly what your browser supports.

        Will it add real detail?

        It sharpens edges and removes blockiness with a small neural network. It cannot recover detail that was never recorded, so expect cleaner lines rather than new texture. Animation and illustration usually look best; photos improve less.

        Why is 4x only for images?

        The network doubles size. For images, running it twice gives 4x. For video that would be far too slow in a browser, so video uses one 2x pass and is capped at 1080p.

        Why was my video refused?

        Videos that are already 1080p or larger, videos stored rotated (many phone recordings), very long files, and codecs your browser cannot decode are refused with the reason shown on screen.

        Why is it slow on my phone?

        Everything runs on the GPU of the device you use. Phones have much less GPU power and memory than a desktop, so keep files short and small, and expect long processing times.
