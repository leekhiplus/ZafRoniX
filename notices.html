Libraries and models

  • WebSR (@websr/websr): the WebGPU runtime that executes the neural network.
  • Anime4K: the CNN upscaling networks that the model weights in this site are based on.
  • Mediabunny: reading and writing MP4 files in the browser.
  • web-demuxer: reading compressed video and audio packets from video files, using a WebAssembly build of FFmpeg's demuxers.

Each of these is distributed under its own license, which is included in its package. The model weight files in /weights come from the reference project above. Their original training and licensing terms should be confirmed with the authors before any commercial use of this site.
